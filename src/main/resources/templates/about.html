About HONK Limousine Service

Chicago's Trusted Ground Transportation Partner

HONK Limousine Service has been providing dependable, professional ground transportation across Chicago and its suburbs. From airport transfers to weddings, corporate travel, and group events, our mission is simple: get you there safely, on time, and in comfort.

Every chauffeur on our team is licensed, background-checked, and trained to deliver a smooth, professional experience from pickup to drop-off. Our fleet is maintained to the highest standard, and our dispatch team is available 24/7 to handle bookings and last-minute changes.
